# AcademyFlow — Commercial & Legal Readiness

Last reviewed: 2026-09-27

## Public legal center

AcademyFlow publishes:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Cancellation & Refund Policy
- Data Deletion Policy
- Cookies & Local Storage Policy
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Support & Complaints Policy
- Data Processing Addendum (DPA)
- Privacy Rights Request form

## Enforced workflows

- Academy owners must accept the current Terms, Privacy Policy and DPA version before using the academy workspace.
- Acceptance version and timestamp are stored on the owner account and audited.
- Data-subject requests receive a tracking number and are managed from the Super Admin console.
- Request types include access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, consent withdrawal, stopping non-essential electronic notifications, and privacy complaints.
- The Super Admin compliance center includes:
  - Record of Processing Activities (RoPA)
  - Personal-data incident register
  - 72-hour notification deadline tracking
- System Health calculates commercial/legal readiness from real platform identity fields.

## Required real-world business details

The software must not invent these values. The owner must enter the real, valid details in Super Admin > Settings:

- Legal entity name
- Commercial Registration number
- E-commerce licence / permit number
- Business address
- Support email
- Support phone
- Privacy / Data Protection Officer name
- Privacy / Data Protection Officer email

The tax number is displayed when applicable.

## Oman references used for the implementation

The implementation was designed with reference to the Omani Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022, as amended), its Executive Regulation (Ministerial Decision 34/2024 and subsequent amendments), the Electronic Transactions Law (Royal Decree 39/2025), and the E-Commerce Regulation (Ministerial Decision 499/2023).

This repository documentation is an operational compliance aid and does not replace advice from qualified Omani legal counsel or the statutory licences, permits, registrations, approvals, or controller obligations that apply to the actual operating entity.
